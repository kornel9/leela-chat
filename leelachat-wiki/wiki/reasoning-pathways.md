# Reasoning pathways

**Summary**: A reasoning pathway is a full-model circuit graph whose nodes are interpretable sparse features and whose edges are measured causal feature-to-feature effects, traced through Lc0/BT4's transformer to explain how it arrives at a particular chess move. It is the mechanistic-interpretability artifact that turns "the network plays Qxh7+" into a step-by-step, causally-verified account of why.

**Sources**: Tracing the Thought of a Grandmaster-level Chess-Playing Transformer.pdf, Sparse Feature Circuits.pdf, Towards Automated Circuit Discovery.pdf, Contrastive Sparse Autoencoders for Interpreting Planning of Chess-Playing Agents.pdf, Understanding the learned look-ahead behavior.pdf, Evidence of Learned Look-Ahead.pdf

## What it is
A reasoning pathway is a subnetwork (a directed graph) inside a chess transformer that is causally responsible for a specific decision — typically choosing one move over alternatives. Instead of coarse units like raw neurons or whole attention heads, the modern formulation uses sparse dictionary features as graph nodes and quantifies each edge by intervening on the network and measuring the downstream change (source: Tracing the Thought of a Grandmaster-level Chess-Playing Transformer.pdf, §3 Constructing Reasoning Pathways; Algorithm 1). This matters for interpreting Lc0/BT4 because it converts the network's opaque single forward pass into an auditable chain of chess-meaningful steps — bishop control transferred across squares, opponent rook coverage incorporated, a defensive dependency over-weighted — each one verifiable by a causal intervention rather than by chess intuition (source: Tracing the Thought of a Grandmaster-level Chess-Playing Transformer.pdf, §4 Findings 1-3).

## In the literature
- **[[fudan-tracing-thought]]** is the chess-specific instantiation and the term's origin for Lc0: it decomposes BOTH the MLP modules (via Transcoders) and the attention modules (via Low-Rank Sparse Attention, Lorsa) of the BT4 network, then builds reasoning pathways by feature steering — adding a feature's decoder direction to the residual stream at scale alpha=-1 (equivalent to zero-ablation) and measuring feature-to-output and feature-to-feature effects (source: Tracing the Thought of a Grandmaster-level Chess-Playing Transformer.pdf, §3, Eq. 5-7; Algorithm 1). In its defensive-fixation case study, zero-ablating the d3→h7 bishop-control attention dropped P(Qxh7+) from 28.75% to 23.35% and stronger steering collapsed it to 0.7%, while ablating an own-pawn-detection feature at g2 flipped P(Qxh7+) from 28.8% to ~91% (source: Tracing the Thought of a Grandmaster-level Chess-Playing Transformer.pdf, §4 Findings 1 and 3; Table 4). Quantitative pathway metrics show Lc0 reasons in parallel: top-2-move path overlap stays below 15% (7.05% overall vs a 0.17% random baseline), and decision-critical information progressively converges onto each move's source and target squares by layer 14 (source: Tracing the Thought of a Grandmaster-level Chess-Playing Transformer.pdf, §5.1-5.2; Table 5).
- **[[sparse-feature-circuits]]** is the methodological precedent (in language models) that the chess work builds on: it defines causal graphs whose nodes are SAE features and whose edges are scored by attribution patching / integrated-gradients patching, avoiding a full intervention per node (source: Sparse Feature Circuits.pdf, §3.1). It shows feature-level circuits are far more efficient than neuron circuits — ~100 feature nodes versus ~1500 neurons to explain subject-verb agreement in Pythia-70M, and ~500 versus ~50,000 for Gemma-2-2B (source: Sparse Feature Circuits.pdf, Fig. 3) — and insists SAE error terms be kept as first-class nodes so unexplained variance is never silently dropped (source: Sparse Feature Circuits.pdf, §2).
- **[[automated-circuit-discovery]]** (ACDC) systematizes the older, coarser version of pathway extraction and automates it: it represents a transformer as a DAG of attention heads, MLPs, and residual-stream edges, then greedily prunes edges whose removal raises KL divergence by less than a threshold (source: Towards Automated Circuit Discovery.pdf, Abstract; Algorithm 1). On GPT-2's Greater-Than circuit it rediscovered all 5/5 component types and selected only 68 of 32,000 edges (source: Towards Automated Circuit Discovery.pdf, Abstract). Two warnings transfer directly to chess: performance is very sensitive to the choice of corrupted (baseline) distribution, and the greedy KL objective systematically misses "negative" components that actively suppress a behavior — relevant if Lc0 has heads that suppress incorrect moves (source: Towards Automated Circuit Discovery.pdf, §4.1).
- **[[contrastive-sae-planning]]** targets the planning level of pathways rather than single-position circuits: it trains Contrastive SAEs on pairs of Leela Chess Zero trajectory states and splits the dictionary into c-features (common, root-state) and d-features (differentiating optimal from suboptimal plans) (source: Contrastive Sparse Autoencoders for Interpreting Planning of Chess-Playing Agents.pdf, §3.1). The partition is validated by entropy (d-features H=3.24 vs c-features H=2.81) and by linear probes for trajectory optimality (F1 0.566 on d-features vs 0.537 on c-features) (source: Contrastive Sparse Autoencoders for Interpreting Planning of Chess-Playing Agents.pdf, Table 1). Note this work studies a 10-layer ResNet/SE LeelaZero, not BT4's transformer, so the methodology transfers but hyperparameters differ (source: Contrastive Sparse Autoencoders for Interpreting Planning of Chess-Playing Agents.pdf, §3.1 and notes).
- **[[evidence-learned-look-ahead]]** supplies the foundational mechanistic components that reasoning pathways recover and extend: using activation patching, attention ablation, and probing on Leela's policy network, it shows the third-move target square is causally disproportionate (patching it at layer 10 cuts correct-move log odds by 1.88 vs at most 0.55 for any other square), that head L12H12 moves information backward in time from the third-move target to the first-move target, and that a bilinear probe predicts the move two turns ahead with 92% accuracy (source: Evidence of Learned Look-Ahead.pdf, §3.1-3.3). It studied the older T82 network and explicitly flags that smolgen complicates attention interpretability (source: Evidence of Learned Look-Ahead.pdf, Appendix A).
- **[[understanding-look-ahead-behavior]]** extends those components deeper into the pathway: it finds the 15-layer Leela policy network encodes board states up to 7 moves ahead (weakening with depth, peaking around layer 13), that L12H12 is a general "backward-in-time" information mover while L12H17 complements it in non-checkmate positions, and that the model considers multiple alternative branches simultaneously rather than a single principal variation (source: Understanding the learned look-ahead behavior.pdf, §3, Figs. 3, 5, 6). It cautions that probing detects opponent-move encoding that patching misses — so probe-based and patching-based pathway extractors give complementary, not redundant, signals (source: Understanding the learned look-ahead behavior.pdf, §3 and Appendix C). The Fudan pathways paper explicitly recovers this learned-look-ahead phenomenon and gives it a "lookahead-to-action" mechanistic account in mate-in-two sacrifice cases (source: Tracing the Thought of a Grandmaster-level Chess-Playing Transformer.pdf, §6 Related Work; Appendix I).

## Relevance to Leela Chat
Reasoning pathways are the literal deliverable of Phase 7 — the project's North Star is to reproduce and productize the Fudan paper, which is built on the exact network the project pins (BT4-1024x15x32h-swa-6147500), so its features and pathways transfer directly rather than needing re-derivation (source: Tracing the Thought of a Grandmaster-level Chess-Playing Transformer.pdf, Appendix A.1-A.2). In the pipeline, a Phase 7 pathway extractor would run pathway construction (Algorithm 1: select significant features as nodes, build edges via causal feature pairs, group into supernodes) and emit addressable feature/edge findings (e.g. `Tc.layer.id@square`, `Lorsa.layer.id@square`), which the LLM translator then narrates as ordered steps — never inventing a step not backed by a measured intervention (source: Tracing the Thought of a Grandmaster-level Chess-Playing Transformer.pdf, §3-4). The feature-steering interventions (add decoder direction at alpha=-1, measure the change in move probability) are also the Phase 6 differential/counterfactual primitive, and ACDC's corrupted-distribution sensitivity is the concrete warning for choosing chess-semantically-appropriate baseline positions in that extractor (source: Towards Automated Circuit Discovery.pdf, §4.1).
