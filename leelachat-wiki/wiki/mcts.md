# MCTS & self-play RL

**Summary**: Monte Carlo Tree Search (MCTS) plus self-play reinforcement learning is the training-and-inference paradigm behind AlphaZero and its open-source descendant Leela Chess Zero (Lc0/BT4): a policy+value network learns from scratch by playing itself, and at decision time a tree search guided by that network produces visit-count statistics that are the primary signals Leela Chat surfaces to users.

**Sources**: A general reinforcement learning algorithm that masters chess.pdf, Mastering Chess and Shogi by Self-Play.pdf, Aligning Superhuman AI with Human Behavior Chess as a Model System.pdf, Contrastive Sparse Autoencoders for Interpreting Planning of Chess-Playing Agents.pdf, Grandmaster-Level Chess Without Search.pdf, Bridging the Human–AI Knowledge Gap.pdf, Evaluation Beyond Task Performance AlphaZero in HEX.pdf

**Last updated**: 2026-06-02

---

## What it is
Self-play RL trains a single dual-head neural network (a policy vector over moves plus a scalar value) starting from random play with only the game rules as input; MCTS is the search procedure that uses that network at decision time, running many simulations that select actions by a PUCT rule and back up network evaluations into per-state-action statistics (source: A general reinforcement learning algorithm that masters chess.pdf, §Search). This matters for interpreting Lc0/BT4 because Lc0 uses the same paradigm: the MCTS statistics {N(s,a), W(s,a), Q(s,a), P(s,a)} are exactly the signals Leela Chat's MCTS extractor must parse from Lc0's verbose output, and the board-encoding details (8-step history, castling/repetition/no-progress planes) determine whether re-analysis FENs reproduce the activations probes were trained on (source: A general reinforcement learning algorithm that masters chess.pdf, project_relevance). The selectivity of network-guided search — strong play despite far fewer evaluated positions — is why Leela Chat treats policy and value estimates as primary explanatory signals rather than treating Lc0 as a brute-force evaluator (source: A general reinforcement learning algorithm that masters chess.pdf, project_relevance).

## In the literature
- **AlphaZero (Science version)** introduces a single self-play algorithm that, given only the rules, beat the 2016 TCEC champion Stockfish 155–6 over 1,000 games at 3-hour controls; each MCTS move during training runs 800 simulations with PUCT action selection over statistics {N, W, Q, P}, and the network evaluates ~60,000 positions/second versus Stockfish's ~58 million, compensating via selective search (source: A general reinforcement learning algorithm that masters chess.pdf, §Results / Fig. 2; §Search; §Discussion / Table S4). It used no opening book, tablebases, or quiescence search and rediscovered common human openings on its own (source: A general reinforcement learning algorithm that masters chess.pdf, §Domain Knowledge / §Results). See [[alphazero-science]].
- **AlphaZero (arXiv preprint)** is the same work and reports that AlphaZero surpassed Stockfish after only 4 hours / 300k training steps, lost zero of 100 games at 1 minute/move, and showed that MCTS with neural-network evaluation scales more effectively with thinking time than alpha-beta search (source: Mastering Chess and Shogi by Self-Play.pdf, Fig. 1; Table 1; Fig. 2 and §MCTS and Alpha-Beta Search). It also documents the board input (T=8 history steps, 119 planes for chess) and the 8×8×73 / 4,672-move policy encoding that underpin Lc0's move-vector layout (source: Mastering Chess and Shogi by Self-Play.pdf, Table S1; Table S2). See [[alphazero-arxiv]].
- **Maia** repurposes the AlphaZero/Leela network to predict human moves and, notably, removes MCTS entirely: the policy head alone predicts the next human move, and the authors find that removing tree search improves human-move prediction by 5–10 percentage points (source: Aligning Superhuman AI with Human Behavior Chess as a Model System.pdf, §4.3, Fig. 5). This is the inverse of AlphaZero's claim that MCTS is what raises play strength — Maia shows MCTS actively hurts the distinct objective of matching human behavior, and reinforces keeping Lc0's MCTS output and network internals as separate signals (source: Aligning Superhuman AI with Human Behavior Chess as a Model System.pdf, §4.3; project_relevance). See [[maia-aligning-superhuman-ai]].
- **Grandmaster-Level Chess Without Search** trains a 270M-parameter transformer via supervised learning (no MCTS, no search) to Lichess blitz Elo 2895; for comparison, AlphaZero without MCTS reaches only Elo 1620 (policy net) or 1853 (value net), whereas AlphaZero with 400 MCTS simulations reaches 2502 (source: Grandmaster-Level Chess Without Search.pdf, Abstract; Table 1). This both quantifies MCTS's contribution to AlphaZero's strength and demonstrates that a search-free network can rival it at scale, validating that Lc0's network-internal value/action signals are meaningful on their own (source: Grandmaster-Level Chess Without Search.pdf, Table 1; project_relevance). See [[grandmaster-chess-without-search]].
- **Contrastive SAEs for planning** trains sparse autoencoders on pairs of LeelaZero MCTS-trajectory states — the root position concatenated with an optimal or a suboptimal follow-up — to disentangle planning features from positional background, splitting the dictionary into common (c) and differentiating (d) features and reaching R²=0.81 reconstruction with ~73 active features (source: Contrastive Sparse Autoencoders for Interpreting Planning of Chess-Playing Agents.pdf, §3.1; Table 4). It uses MCTS trajectories directly as the contrastive signal, mapping the optimal-vs-suboptimal rollout distinction onto interpretable planning concepts (source: Contrastive Sparse Autoencoders for Interpreting Planning of Chess-Playing Agents.pdf, §3.1, project_relevance). See [[contrastive-sae-planning]].
- **Bridging the Human–AI Knowledge Gap** uses AlphaZero's MCTS statistics to define dynamic concepts — positive examples are the highest-visit rollout, negatives are suboptimal rollouts at multiple MCTS depths — and learns sparse concept vectors from as few as 10 labelled examples, then teaches them to four grandmasters (rated 2600–2800) who all improved on concept puzzles by +6 to +42 percentage points (source: Bridging the Human–AI Knowledge Gap.pdf, §4.1.2 / Equations 3-5; §5.1.2 / Fig. 6; Table 4 / §6.1). The optimal-vs-suboptimal MCTS rollout contrast it relies on is exactly the evidence Leela Chat's why_this_move and did_you_consider handlers already extract (source: Bridging the Human–AI Knowledge Gap.pdf, project_relevance). See [[bridging-human-ai-knowledge-gap]].
- **AlphaZero in Hex** probes and behaviorally tests an AlphaZero agent and finds that MCTS behaviorally uses concepts before the network's internal representations encode them, with both converging near the end of training, and that short-term concepts sit in the final layers while long-term concepts peak in the middle layers (source: Evaluation Beyond Task Performance AlphaZero in HEX.pdf, Abstract, §4.3, Fig. 6; §4.2, Fig. 4b). This implies MCTS visit-share signals can carry strategic information the residual stream does not yet encode, so MCTS-derived features are not redundant with network probes (source: Evaluation Beyond Task Performance AlphaZero in HEX.pdf, project_relevance). See [[alphazero-hex-evaluation]].

## Relevance to Leela Chat
This concept anchors Phase 2 (MCTS Integration), where extractors pull value estimates and the visit-count distribution {N, W, Q, P} out of Lc0's MCTS tree to feed the findings JSON that handlers assemble for the translator (source: A general reinforcement learning algorithm that masters chess.pdf, project_relevance; Mastering Chess and Shogi by Self-Play.pdf, project_relevance). The MCTS scaling result — more thinking time yields meaningfully better play rather than diminishing returns — underpins ADR-0012's deep-mode design, and the worked MCTS diagram is a concrete template for what the MCTS handler's JSON should contain (source: Mastering Chess and Shogi by Self-Play.pdf, project_relevance; A general reinforcement learning algorithm that masters chess.pdf, project_relevance). Across the pipeline, the optimal-vs-suboptimal rollout contrast supplies grounded evidence for why_this_move and did_you_consider handlers (Phase 6 differential analysis), while the encoding details (castling, en passant, repetition, no-progress) are validator-relevant: FEN metadata must be preserved or evaluations silently corrupt — a pitfall already flagged in CLAUDE.md §8 (source: Bridging the Human–AI Knowledge Gap.pdf, project_relevance; Grandmaster-Level Chess Without Search.pdf, §5; Mastering Chess and Shogi by Self-Play.pdf, project_relevance).
